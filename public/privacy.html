1. Scope and who we are

This Policy explains how Invoqly (“Invoqly,” “we,” “us”) handles personal information when you visit our website, join the beta, create an account, use invoicing features or contact support. Invoqly determines how information is used for operating the service. Business customers may separately be responsible for personal information they place in invoices.

2. Account and identity information

We collect information you provide, including your name, work email, password credentials handled by our authentication provider, language preference, business name, logo and account settings. If you join a waitlist or request support, we also collect the information contained in that request and our response history.

3. Sign-in and account security

Invoqly uses Supabase to create accounts and authenticate email-and-password sign-ins. Supabase processes account credentials, confirmation links, password-reset requests and session tokens on our behalf. Invoqly does not display your password to other users, and passwords are handled through the authentication provider rather than stored in readable form in the website. We use authentication information only to identify your account, secure sessions, prevent misuse and provide support.

4. Business, client and invoice data

To provide invoicing features, we may process business contact details, tax or registration identifiers, client names and addresses, invoice numbers, line items, currencies, prices, taxes, payment instructions, due dates, notes, logos and invoice status. You decide what information to enter and must ensure you are permitted to process and share it. Avoid entering sensitive information that is not necessary for invoicing.

5. Technical and usage information

When you use Invoqly, we and our infrastructure providers may automatically receive IP address, browser and device type, operating system, approximate location derived from IP, page visits, referring pages, timestamps, language, cookie or local-storage identifiers, diagnostic events and security logs. We use this data to deliver pages, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse and understand product performance.

6. Purposes and legal bases

We process information to perform our agreement with you by creating accounts and delivering invoicing features; for legitimate interests such as securing, maintaining and improving the service, preventing fraud and communicating about the beta; with consent where required; and to comply with legal obligations or protect legal rights. Where local law recognizes other legal bases, we rely on the basis appropriate to the activity.

7. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We disclose it to service providers acting for us; to another user or client when you intentionally send or share an invoice; to professional advisers under confidentiality; in connection with financing, restructuring, acquisition or sale, subject to appropriate safeguards; or where reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce agreements, investigate fraud or protect people, rights and systems.

8. Service providers

Cloudflare hosts, delivers and protects the website. Supabase provides email-and-password authentication and may provide database, storage and related backend services. We may add providers for email delivery, error monitoring, product analytics or customer support as the beta develops. We require providers to process information for defined services and apply appropriate safeguards.

9. International data transfers

Invoqly is intended for businesses in multiple countries. Information may be processed in the United Arab Emirates, the United States, the European Economic Area and other locations where we or our providers operate. Where required, transfers are supported by contractual protections, adequacy decisions or other lawful safeguards.

10. Retention

We keep account and service information while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to support restoration, security, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Invoice and transaction-related records may need to be retained longer where tax, accounting or legal rules apply. Security logs and backups are retained for limited operational cycles. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed, unless law requires retention.

11. Security

We use practical administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed for an early-stage cloud service, including HTTPS, managed authentication, access controls, provider security features and separation of public and secret credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a unique password, protect your email account and devices, and notify us promptly of suspected misuse.

12. Cookies and local storage

We use essential cookies or browser storage to maintain authentication sessions, protect accounts, remember language and interface preferences and keep the service working. Infrastructure providers may set security or load-balancing cookies. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies or sell browsing profiles. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, this Policy and any consent controls will be updated first.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal information; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; and complain to a data-protection authority. These rights may be limited by identity verification, legal exceptions and record-retention duties. To make a request, email hamzahfreelancingwala@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

14. Account deletion and authentication data

You may request deletion of your Invoqly account and associated personal information by emailing hamzahfreelancingwala@gmail.com. Deleting an account ends future sign-in and removes or de-identifies eligible profile information after we verify the request. It does not automatically remove records that must be retained for security, tax, dispute resolution or other legal purposes. Authentication logs and backups may remain for limited operational periods before being overwritten.

15. Children

Invoqly is a business invoicing service and is not directed to children. Users must be able to enter a binding agreement in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum legal age. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

16. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy as the product, providers or laws change. The effective date above shows the latest version. For material changes, we will provide reasonable notice through the service or by email where appropriate. For questions, privacy requests or complaints, contact hamzahfreelancingwala@gmail.com.

17. Information about invoice recipients

A business user may enter information about clients, contacts and invoice recipients. In that situation, the business user normally decides why the information is used and is responsible for giving required notices and having a lawful basis to provide it to Invoqly. We process the information to create, display, translate, store, export or deliver the invoice as instructed. Recipients may contact the issuing business about invoice accuracy and may contact us about how Invoqly processes their information.

18. Email and service communications

We may use your email address for account verification, sign-in, security alerts, invoice activity, support replies, important beta notices and changes to this Policy or the Terms. These operational messages are necessary to provide and protect an account. Optional announcements or marketing messages, if introduced, will identify an unsubscribe method. Unsubscribing from optional messages does not stop security, legal or transactional communications.

19. Public links, exports and downloads

If you choose to create a shareable invoice link, download a file or send an invoice, the selected information leaves the private account view and becomes available through the channel you choose. Anyone who receives a public or forwarded link may be able to view its contents. Use recipient restrictions when offered, share links carefully and remove access when it is no longer needed. Files downloaded to a device are controlled by the person or organization holding that device.

20. Aggregated and de-identified information

We may combine usage information or remove details that reasonably identify a person or business to understand service performance, measure feature adoption, plan capacity and improve reliability. We do not attempt to re-identify information that has been properly de-identified. Aggregated statistics may be retained longer because they no longer describe an identifiable user, provided they remain in that form.

21. Automated formatting and calculations

Invoqly may use software rules to format invoices, calculate displayed totals, select layouts or flag incomplete information. These functions support document preparation and do not make legal, credit, employment or other decisions that produce similarly significant effects about you or an invoice recipient. Users must review output before sending it. If future features materially change this position, we will update this Policy and provide any controls required by law.

22. Browser signals and advertising

Invoqly does not currently sell personal information, serve behaviourally targeted advertising or build advertising profiles from invoice content. Because there is no consistent industry standard for browser “Do Not Track” signals, the service does not currently respond to them as a separate control. If advertising or cross-site tracking is introduced, we will update this Policy and provide notices and choices required for affected users.

23. Business changes and legal requests

If Invoqly is financed, reorganized, merged, acquired or transferred, information may be reviewed or transferred as part of that transaction under confidentiality and appropriate safeguards. We may preserve or disclose information when we reasonably believe it is required by valid legal process, necessary to protect rights or safety, or needed to investigate fraud and abuse. Where lawful and practical, we will direct requests to the relevant business customer or notify the affected user.

24. Questions, complaints and contact

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, authentication questions or complaints, email hamzahfreelancingwala@gmail.com from the address associated with your account where possible. Describe the request and the account or invoice involved without emailing unnecessary sensitive information. We may ask for information needed to verify identity and protect the account. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may have the right to complain to the privacy or data-protection authority where you live.

25. Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your account identifier, email, name and profile image to Supabase for authentication. Invoqly uses this information to create or identify your account and display your profile. Basic sign-in does not request Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts. We do not sell Google-derived data or use it for advertising. You may revoke access in your Google Account settings; revocation and deletion of your Invoqly account are separate actions. Use and transfer of Google API data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
